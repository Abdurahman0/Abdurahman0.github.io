Channel Upload Tool

A private tool that the owner of two YouTube channels uses to upload and schedule his own videos.

Who it is for: only the channel owner. It is not offered to the public, there is no sign-up, and nobody else can connect an account to it.

Channels: UNREALITY LAB and Unsaid (@unsaidteens), both owned and run by the same person.

What it does

  • Uploads the owner's own finished videos to his own channels, first as private.
  • Sets the title, description, thumbnail and the scheduled publish time.
  • Reads the channel's own upload list to check that each video went public on time.

How it uses Google data

The tool signs in with Google (OAuth) and uses the YouTube Data API only for the steps above, on the owner's own channels. It does not read anyone else's data, does not show ads and does not share or sell any data. Details are in the Privacy Policy.
